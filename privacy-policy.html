Privacy Policy

Cornicello Caffè respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you contact us, request a quote, visit our website, or work with us.

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Information We Collect

We collect the information you choose to share with us, such as your name, email address, phone number, business name, event details, service needs, and messages.

Our website host or basic analytics tools may also collect technical information, such as browser type, device information, IP address, pages visited, and general usage data.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to inquiries and prepare quotes or service recommendations.
  • To schedule installations, restocking, events, service visits, and follow-up support.
  • To manage client relationships, billing records, and business administration.
  • To improve our website, services, communications, and customer experience.

Sharing and Retention

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us host the website, communicate with clients, provide services, process records, or meet legal requirements.

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described here, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to legal and business record requirements. You may also withdraw consent for certain uses where applicable.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal information. No website, email, or digital system is completely risk-free, so please avoid sending highly sensitive information through ordinary email.
