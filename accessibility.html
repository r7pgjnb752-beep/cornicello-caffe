Accessibility

Cornicello Caffè wants our website and service information to be easy to reach, read, and use. We aim to improve accessibility as the site grows and welcome feedback from visitors.

Our Commitment

We are working to make the Cornicello Caffè website usable for as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology, keyboard navigation, zoom, or mobile devices.

Website Practices

  • Responsive page layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
  • Semantic page structure, headings, links, and button labels.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images where practical.
  • Keyboard-friendly links, buttons, and contact actions.
  • Colour contrast and readable type choices considered throughout the design.

Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. As we add pages, images, forms, or third-party tools, we will continue reviewing the site and making reasonable improvements.

Requesting Help

If you need website information in another format, or if something is difficult to access, contact us by email or phone and we will do our best to help.
